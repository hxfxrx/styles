# Resources for learning Dutch (A0 → A2)

Free unless marked otherwise. You don't need all of these — pick **one app, one podcast, one
video source**, and rotate.

## Flashcards (daily, from week 1)

- **Anki** — make your own deck from the module vocabulary lists (best retention), or search
  shared decks for "Dutch 1000 most common words".
- **Quizlet / Memrise** — friendlier interfaces, decent ready-made Dutch courses.

## Apps and structured courses

- **Duolingo Dutch** — fine as a daily warm-up; don't let it be your only study.
- **DutchPod101** — audio lessons by level; the A1/A2 dialogues pair well with this course.
- **Universiteit Groningen "Introduction to Dutch"** (FutureLearn) — free 3-week MOOC,
  excellent companion to Modules 1–2.
- **Nedles / LearnDutch.org (Bart de Pau)** — "1000 most common words in 40 lessons" video
  series on YouTube is a classic; his grammar playlists map well to Modules 1–5.

## Listening (from week 3)

- **Zeg het in het Nederlands** (podcast) — slow, clear Dutch about everyday topics; built
  for A1–A2 learners.
- **Een Beetje Nederlands** (podcast) — slow Dutch with transcripts; ideal from Module 4.
- **NOS Jeugdjournaal** (TV/YouTube) — daily news for Dutch kids; clear speech, real content.
  Start in Module 5; expect to understand fragments at first, that's normal.
- **Het Klokhuis** — kids' educational TV; short topical episodes.

## Reading (from week 5)

- **Nieuwsbegrip / "nieuws in makkelijke taal"**: try **Eenvoudig Communiceren / Lees je in**
  graded readers and the site **wablieft.be** (Belgian easy-language newspaper).
- **Zoek de vertaling niet meteen op**: read once for gist, once with a dictionary.
- Graded readers: the **"Leeslicht"** and **"Boekenpret NT2"** series (paid, worth it) have
  A1/A2 novellas.

## Watching

- Dutch Netflix/streaming shows with **Dutch subtitles** (not English!): start Module 5+.
  Easy picks: *De Luizenmoeder* (comedy), kids' series, or anything you've already seen
  dubbed/subbed in Dutch.
- **NPO Start** (Dutch public broadcaster) and **VRT MAX** (Flanders) — free with account,
  may need a VPN outside the Benelux.

## Speaking practice (the A2 accelerator)

- **iTalki / Preply** (paid) — 30-minute "conversation only" lessons with a community tutor,
  1–2× per week from Module 3 onward, is the single highest-impact spend.
- **Tandem / HelloTalk** (free) — language-exchange chat with Dutch speakers learning your
  language; agree on half Dutch, half your language.
- If you're in NL/BE: **taalcafés** (language cafés) at public libraries are free and
  welcoming to beginners.

## Dictionaries and tools

- **Van Dale online** / **woorden.org** — Dutch dictionaries.
- **DeepL / Google Translate** — fine for checking your writing direction NL→EN; be skeptical
  EN→NL (it produces B2-level phrasing you can't yet verify).
- **welklidwoord.nl** — instantly answers "de of het?".
- **Verbix / mijnwoordenboek.nl** — full verb conjugation tables.

## Official exams (optional, after week 12)

- **CNaVT** (Certificaat Nederlands als Vreemde Taal) — internationally recognized; the
  "Maatschappij informeel (INFO)" exam targets A2.
- **Inburgeringsexamen** (NL civic-integration exam) — its language parts are A2; relevant if
  you're settling in the Netherlands. From abroad: *basisexamen inburgering* (A1) precedes it.
- Many Dutch **volksuniversiteiten** and Belgian **CVO**s offer cheap placement tests if you
  just want an honest level check without a formal certificate.
